FAQ

Before you write

What can I contact you about?

ICT services and support, questions about our articles and tutorials, feedback, and partnership or advertising enquiries.

How soon will I get a reply?

You will see an on-page confirmation as soon as your message is sent. We reply by email, so please use an address you check often.

How do I get faster help with a problem?

Describe what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and the device or browser you use. Screenshots described in words help too.

Is my information safe?

Your details are used only to respond to your enquiry. Read our privacy policy for more.
