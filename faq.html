Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is ScottVentures?

    ScottVentures is a tech blog covering hardware explainers, coding tricks, and hands-on tutorials — written to be short, direct, and actually useful, rather than padded out to hit a word count.

  • How often do you post new content?

    New articles go up regularly. You can always see the latest on the Articles page, and each article has a like/dislike button and a comment thread if you want to weigh in.

  • Do I need an account to read articles?

    No — articles, tutorials, and learning materials are open to everyone. Creating an account just lets you like/dislike articles, leave comments under your name, and get a personalized experience.

  • How do I reset my password?

    On the login page, click "Forgot password?" and enter your account email. You'll get a link to set a new password. Reset links expire after 30 minutes for security.

  • Can I write a guest post or suggest a topic?

    Yes — that's exactly what the contact page is for. Tell us what you have in mind and we'll get back to you.

  • How do the like/dislike buttons and comments work?

    Every article has a like/dislike button and a comment section at the bottom. You don't need an account to vote or comment — votes are tracked per browser, and comments just ask for a name.

  • Is my personal information safe?

    Passwords are never stored in plain text, and contact messages are only used to respond to you. We don't sell or share your information. See the About page for more on how the site is run.

  • I found a bug or something isn't working — what do I do?

    Please let us know through the contact form with as much detail as you can — what you were doing, what happened, and what browser or device you're using. That's the fastest way to get it fixed.

Still have a question?

If you didn't find what you were looking for, reach out directly and we'll get back to you.
