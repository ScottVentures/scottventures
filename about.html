About ScottVentures

ScottVentures started as a running list of "wait, how does that actually work?" moments — the kind that come up while building something, fixing something, or just poking around a machine out of curiosity. Most of those questions had answers buried in forum threads, twenty-minute videos, or documentation written for people who already knew the answer.

This site is the version we wished existed: short, direct, and written for someone who's curious but doesn't have twenty minutes to spare. Every post aims to leave you with something you can actually use — a concept that finally clicks, or a trick that saves real time.

What you'll find here

  • Hardware explainers that go deeper than the basics
  • Coding tricks and terminal shortcuts worth remembering
  • Hands-on tutorials when a topic needs more room
  • Occasional deep dives when five minutes isn't enough

What you won't find

  • Padded intros before getting to the point
  • Affiliate-driven "best of" roundups with nothing tested
  • Content stretched out to hit a word count
  • 400-word topics padded into 2,000-word posts

Got a topic idea?

If there's something you've always wanted explained clearly, or you want to pitch a guest post, the contact page is the fastest way to reach us — or check the FAQ first, it might already be answered there.
