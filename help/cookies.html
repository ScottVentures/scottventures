Cookies

Local storage, not tracking cookies

ScottVentures itself doesn't set tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to remember your login session and the Display and Accessibility preferences you set (dark mode, text size). That data stays in your browser — it isn't used for advertising and isn't shared with third parties.

What local storage holds

  • Session — so you stay logged in between visits instead of signing in every time.
  • Theme — whether you've switched to dark mode.
  • Language — the footer language switcher's last choice.

You can clear any of this at any time through your browser's own site-data settings; clearing it just logs you out and resets those preferences to default.

Third-party cookies

The one exception is the ad script on this site (Google AdSense), which may set its own cookies to serve and measure ads, under Google's own policies rather than ours. We don't control what that script stores, and you can manage ad personalization through your Google account settings or browser ad controls.

More on data handling

This page covers cookies and local storage specifically. For how account details, comments, and contact-form messages are handled, see the full Privacy Policy.
